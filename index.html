Victoria Przydanek

I am a University of Pennsylvania student under the Vagelos Integrated Program in Energy Research (VIPER) dual degree program majoring in Electrical Engineering and Physics.

During my time working in academic research labs and Consolidated Edison (energy company serving New York City and Westchester), I have gained keen interests in the optimization of renewable resources and the electric grid; utilizing biomimicry as inspiration for robotic development; and innovating biomedical solutions.

Outside of school, I am a project lead at Penn ADAPT (Assistive Devices and Prosthetic Technologies), audio and light engineer for the Performing Arts Council, and serve as the Social Chair for the VIPER Student Board. In my free time, you can catch me training for a half-marathon, reading bildungsroman, or knitting.
